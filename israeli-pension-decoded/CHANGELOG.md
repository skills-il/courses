- Fixed a material error in Chapter 1: the course stated a "pension-eligible salary cap" of ₪50,695/month for employees. No such cap exists. The 2008 extension order bases mandatory contributions on the LOWER of the determining salary or the average wage (₪13,769 in 2026). The row was replaced with an accurate explanation, and the comprehensive-fund monthly deposit ceiling (₪5,645.29, being 20.5% of twice the average wage) was added in its place, along with what happens to deposits above it.

The mandate is 18.5% in total, 6% employee and 12.5% employer (6.5% tagmulim + 6% pitzuyim). The 8.33% rate is an employer election. The worked example was recomputed for all three cases.
- Fixed an error in Chapter 2: the course said an employee with no prior coverage is mandatory "from month 7, retroactive to month 1 of the tax year". There is no such retroactivity. Entitlement begins only after six months at that employer with no back-payment for that period. Retroactivity to day one belongs to the employee who arrives with active coverage.

שחרור ראשוני של הקורס. שישה פרקים מכסים את מערכת הפנסיה הישראלית עבור שכירים ועצמאים: השלושה־במוצר־אחד, עץ ההחלטה בשתי שאלות, ארבע קרנות ברירת המחדל 2021-2028, איך לקרוא את פנסיה נט, חובת הפנסיה לעצמאים מ־2017 וסולם הטבות המס, ומתי באמת כדאי להחליף קרן. כל הנתונים מעוגנים לשנת 2026.

### English
Initial release. Six chapters covering the Israeli pension system for both employees and self-employed: the three-in-one product, the two-question decision tree, the four 2021-2028 default funds, how to read pensianet, the 2017 self-employed mandate and tax benefit ladder, and when to actually switch funds. All numbers anchored to 2026.
